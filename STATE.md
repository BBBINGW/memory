## MAIN OBSTRUCTION

The exact digit lattice L contains the plaintext modulus p. Reducing to standard mod-q BGV ring when p,q are coprime turns p into an invertible coefficient, hence the full image ideal is the UNIT IDEAL. This kills the simplest attempt to build a dBGV by replacing rational module K[B]/L with mod-q ring quotient. Ordinary BGV decrypt uses finite mod-q ciphertexts and an explicit modp error convention, not a ring homomorphism from Zq to Zp in the coprime case. Mixed-domain lifted digit/carry representations may avoid this, but proving a coherent mod-q arithmetic and `p e` error recurrence with small growth/valid switching is substantial, and source §3 footnote openly notes direct BGV unknown. Cannot convert this obstruction alone to paper-worthy improvement.
