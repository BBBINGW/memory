# BOOT-B068 — Scale-preserving encrypted phase doubling: native negacyclic no-go, odd-cyclotomic Galois escape

## Primary hypothesis
From the B067 single-blind-rotation GLWE encryption of Delta*X^t, can we derive same-scale GLWE encryption Delta*X^(2t) for every t in Z_(2N) by an inexpensive public/linear transform instead of another PBS or general ct-ct square? Find decisive algebra/type evidence and one well-scoped alternative ring interface.

## Exact cheapest no-go for additive cheap interface
[DERIVATION] For negacyclic monomials v_t=Delta X^t and desired outputs y_t=Delta X^(2t), v_(t+N)=-v_t while y_(t+N)=y_t. Any additive homomorphism F with F(v_t)=y_t for all 2N phases implies y_t=F(-v_t)=-F(v_t)=-y_t, hence 2Delta=0 modQ. This FAILS whenever Q=2^w, Delta=2^(w-b), b>=2; it fails also for odd q with invertible Delta. No linear transform / NTT / public coefficient permutation can realize this exact same-representation mapping for ALL phases. Excludes no nonlinear gate, scheme switch or phase-domain restriction.
[DERIVATION] Substitution X->X² is NOT a ring endomorphism of R_Q unless 2=0, since image of relation X^N+1 is X^(2N)+1=2 in R_Q.
[TEST] Exact negacyclic contradiction across 48 phase-pairs in 4 configurations: N8/Q257/Delta1; N8/Q65536/Delta4096; N16/Q65536/Delta16; N16/Q2^32/Delta2^24. All checked.

## Torus square cannot be linearly repaired after modular reduction
[DERIVATION] Raw ring multiplication produces Delta²*X^(2t) mod Q. When Delta=2^(w-b) with b<=w/2, Delta²=0 modulo 2^w; the phase is literally erased. Even when b>w/2 and Delta² survives, no same-modulus scalar u satisfies u Delta²=Delta modQ: gcd(Delta²,Q) does not divide Delta for any 0<w-b<w. In fact the target has greater additive order, so no additive homomorphism on the subgroup generated by the raw squared term can restore the amplitude. BFV-style extended-precision tensor multiplication and scale-and-round is *not* such a map and is NOT ruled out.
[TEST] Six cases (w,b)=(16,4),(16,12),(32,8),(64,8),(64,16),(64,32) confirm linear-rescale no-go; five of six yield exactly zero raw squared plaintext.

## Characteristic-2/Frobenius escape loses full domain
[DERIVATION] Over F2, polynomial squaring is Frobenius and an F2-linear map, but -1=1, so X^(t+N)=X^t. Full-domain outputs f(t)!=f(t+N) cannot be represented.
[TEST] N=8,16,32: 500 random Frobenius checks per N, all 1500/1500 PASS; all 56 phase pairs t/t+N collapse.

## Positive alternative: odd cyclotomic Galois automorphism
[DERIVATION] In R=F_257[X]/Phi_17(X), gcd(2,17)=1; tau_2:X->X² is a valid ring automorphism. Hence tau_2(Delta X^t)=Delta X^(2t) exactly preserving Delta. For symbolic RLWE ct=(a,b) under secret s with b=a*s+Delta X^t, transforming the two public components yields dec under tau_2(s) equal to Delta X^(2t), but does NOT decrypt under the original s. Requires Galois KS with noise/key costs.
[TEST] Delta7, all 17 t values, 20 random masks per t: 340/340 algebraic decryptions under transformed secret PASS, 340/340 original-secret decryptions mismatch. This is noiseless symbolic toy algebra, NOT native RLWE security or native key switching.
[PRIOR] Wang et al. 2025 already achieves arbitrary full-domain LUT with ONE blind rotation in an odd-prime cyclotomic ring, so adding a 10-key-switch dyadic power chain currently lacks a proven benefit.

## Scale-preserving BFV candidate: budget gate only
For N=1024, B067 dyadic monomial powers need 10 dependent ct-ct squarings in a BFV/BGV/CKKS-like representation. Each needs real multiplication, relinearization and scaling/modulus support, plus TFHE-to-leveled ciphertext representation conversion. Simple plaintext-only oracle z_(j+1)=round(z_j²/Delta), Delta=2^56, z0=Delta+e0, repeated 10 times yields error N*e0 for e0=1,7,100,100000; checked (1024,7168,102400,102400000). This oracle decrypts and divides known plaintext scalars, NOT encrypted evaluation or a certified DFR. First-order relative noise grows >=2^10 in this fixed-point chain, before multiplication/relinearization noises.
Converting LWE to GGSW via circuit bootstrapping itself entails PBS and key switching, not a free GLWE-to-GGSW cast: https://docs.rs/sunscreen_tfhe/latest/sunscreen_tfhe/ops/bootstrapping/fn.circuit_bootstrap_via_trace_and_scheme_switch.html . No measured advantage over one more PBS.
