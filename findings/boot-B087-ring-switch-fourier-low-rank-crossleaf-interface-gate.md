## Exact finite-field theorem under limited interface
Let q=97 (prime), m in {8,16,32}, with m dividing q−1 and primitive omega of order m in Fq. Each f:Z_m→Fq has exact Fourier expansion f(t)=sum_k fhat[k]*omega^(k*t), fhat[k]=m^-1 sum_t f(t) omega^(-k*t). If and ONLY IF the hypothetical leaves encode a,b with original phase t=a+b modulo m, then
 f(a+b)=sum_{k in supp fhat} fhat[k]*omega^(k*a)*omega^(k*b).
The m×m matrix M_f[a,b]=f(a+b mod m) equals V diag(fhat) V^T with invertible finite-field DFT V[a,k]=omega^(k*a). Consequently
  rank_Fq(M_f)=number of nonzero Fourier coefficients.
An online *one-round separable bilinear* representation with r terms sum_j g_j(a) h_j(b) requires r≥rank(M_f); Fourier expansion attains equality. This is NOT a lower bound for arbitrary FHE circuits, FHE keys, interactive rounds or alternative leaf encodings.

Independent additive leaf processing f(a+b)=g(a)+h(b) requires every mixed finite difference to vanish. For one harmonic f(t)=omega^t, difference omega^(a+b)−omega^a−omega^b+1=(omega^a−1)(omega^b−1), nonzero for a=b=1. For m16 the toy gives 225/256 nonzero differences, witness 49 mod97.
