A FREE remapping of encrypted rotation index under unchanged CKKS cyclic HMuxRot must yield Π T_j=T_phi(j) Π for all vectors and secret indices, equivalent to ΠT_jΠ^-1 being a CYCLIC ROTATION. ## Positive full-public-column exception, but costly
SHIP's pure Column Algorithm2 receives a PUBLIC input vector. For each candidate j, precompute ΠT_jv and publish encryption of Πmask_j, weighted by the same encrypted one-hot bit. Linearity and Hadamard slot masks ensure resulting ciphertext semantics equals Π of the original selected rotation WITHOUT an extra online general Π operator. It is a known one-hot column mechanism, not a new primitive; does not solve the original compact hybrid Mux case (its second rotation acts on ENCRYPTED ciphertext).
