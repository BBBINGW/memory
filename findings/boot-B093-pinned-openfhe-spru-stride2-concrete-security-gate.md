## Exact public ring reduction and secret family
With stride t dividing both N and B=N/h, s(X)=s'(X^t), R_p=F_p[X]/(X^N+1), R'_p=F_p[Y]/(Y^(N/t)+1), Y=X^t. The public KSK auxiliary limb b=-a*s+e mod p decomposes:
 a=Σ_(r=0..t-1) X^r a_r(Y), b=Σ_r X^r b_r(Y), e=Σ_r X^r e_r(Y).
Thus b_r(Y)=-a_r(Y)s'(Y)+e_r(Y) mod (p,Y^(N/t)+1) for all r. Uniform a over R_p makes residue-class a_r independent uniform R'_p polys; pure split does not increase error coefficients. The t samples share a single s', with independent coefficient noise but their n' negacyclic scalar expansions have structured dependencies; do not incorrectly model all scalar equations independent.
Block-onehot secret: N32768,h64,B512, first nonzero at index0 PUBLIC by construction, remaining 63 nonzero positions independently one per distinct block, with B/t options each. True support cardinality (B/t)^63 and log2 support for t1/2/4/8/16 = 567/504/441/378/315. The paper's May-like S^(1/4) *lower-bound-style diagnostic* gives 141.75/126/110.25/94.5/78.75; sub-128 diagnostic is NOT proof of an attack or security failure.
