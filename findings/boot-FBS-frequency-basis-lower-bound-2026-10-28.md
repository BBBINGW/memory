For a real-valued target function approximated by

    p(m) = c0 + sum_{k=1}^d ck (z^k + z^{-k}),
    z = omega^m,

2119 directly blind-rotates each positive frequency z^k, so the zero-extra-level version uses d blind-rotation instances.

If one additional multiplication level is available, the paper follows the SWIFT/APSI idea:
- choose a seed exponent set A;
- blind-rotate only z^a for a in A;
- require every k in [d] to be either a seed or representable as a+b with a,b in A;
- recover z^(a+b)=z^a z^b with one ciphertext multiplication.

The paper identifies this as an order-two Global Postage-Stamp Problem (GPSP), reducing the blind-rotation width from d to Theta(sqrt(d)) seeds.

APSI Definition 1 gives the general Global Postage-Stamp Problem:

Given h and k, choose k positive integers A_k={a_1=1<a_2<...<a_k} so that every integer 1,...,n can be expressed as a sum of h or fewer elements of A_k, maximizing n.

The paper explicitly states that if encrypted powers y^{a_i} are supplied, then all powers through n can be computed with multiplicative depth

    ceil(log2 h).

Thus:
    GENERAL_MULTI_LEVEL_SEED_POWER_TRADEOFF = PRIOR ART.

The generic idea "allow L>1 multiplication levels to reduce seed blind rotations further" is therefore not novel by itself.

## Seed-frequency reconstruction model

Consider the 2119-style FBS model:

1. A set A={a_1,...,a_m} of positive frequency exponents is generated directly through expensive blind rotations, giving ciphertexts z^{a_i}.
2. New monomial ciphertexts are generated by ciphertext-ciphertext multiplication.
3. At most L sequential multiplication layers are allowed.
4. Public scalar multiplication, addition, and conjugation are free for the exponent-generation count.
5. The goal is to materialize every positive monomial z^k for 1<=k<=d.

This is narrower than arbitrary evaluation of the final trigonometric polynomial. The lower bound below applies only to this seed-frequency reconstruction model.

## Support-size lemma

A monomial produced at multiplicative depth L can have an exponent that is a sum of at most

    h = 2^L

seed exponents, counted with repetition.

Proof:
- depth 0 monomials use one seed;
- multiplying a depth-L1 monomial and a depth-L2 monomial adds their exponents and their seed multiplicities;
- at depth L, a fan-in-two multiplication tree has at most 2^L leaves.

Conjugation permits sign changes but does not increase the number of seed terms. Since the target positive frequencies 1,...,d can be counted using nonnegative combinations in the GPSP construction, the following counting bound suffices.

## Counting lower bound on the number of blind-rotation seeds

The number of multisets of at most h elements selected from m seed types is

    sum_{j=0}^h binom(m+j-1,j)
      = binom(m+h,h).

Each such multiset determines at most one exponent sum. Distinct multisets may collide, so this is an upper bound on the number of distinct frequencies representable.

To cover all d positive exponents plus zero, a necessary condition is therefore

    binom(m+h,h) >= d+1.          (1)

For fixed h,

    m = Omega(d^(1/h)).

With h=2^L,

    m = Omega(d^(1/2^L)).         (2)

A convenient explicit bound follows from

    binom(m+h,h) <= [e(m+h)/h]^h.

Equation (1) implies

    m >= (h/e)(d+1)^(1/h) - h.

The asymptotic statement is what matters here.

## One-extra-level corollary

For L=1, h=2.

Equation (1) becomes

    binom(m+2,2) >= d+1.

Thus

    m >= (sqrt(8d+9)-3)/2
      = Omega(sqrt(d)).

2119's one-level order-two GPSP construction uses Theta(sqrt(d)) seed frequencies.

Therefore:

    ONE_EXTRA_MULTIPLICATION_LEVEL:
    THETA(sqrt(d)) DIRECT BLIND ROTATIONS IS ASYMPTOTICALLY OPTIMAL

within the seed-frequency reconstruction model.

The result is asymptotic and model-specific; it does not prove the concrete constant in 2119's chosen postage-stamp basis is optimal.

## General L interpretation

With L extra multiplication layers, a best possible seed count in this model can scale no better than

    Omega(d^(1/2^L)).

APSI already supplies the general h-stamp framework with depth ceil(log2 h), so pursuing higher-order GPSP bases for 2119-style FBS is an application of existing generic prior art rather than a new cryptographic mechanism.

It could still be a legitimate engineering choice for a concrete implementation:
- fewer blind rotations;
- more ciphertext multiplications;
- different key/noise/modulus costs.

But a paper whose main contribution is only "use order-h GPSP instead of order-two GPSP in 2119" would not meet the current CCF-A/B novelty bar.

## Important model caveat

This lower bound assumes the algorithm reconstructs all needed monomials z^1,...,z^d individually from seed monomials.

It does NOT rule out:
- evaluating the target trigonometric polynomial in a factored form without materializing every frequency;
- exploiting special coefficient structure of a particular function;
- baby-step/giant-step linear-combination tricks that do not require all monomials independently;
- a different representation in which one blind rotation exposes multiple algebraically useful frequencies at once.

Therefore this is a closure of the obvious frequency-seed/GPSP extension, not a universal lower bound for FBS.
