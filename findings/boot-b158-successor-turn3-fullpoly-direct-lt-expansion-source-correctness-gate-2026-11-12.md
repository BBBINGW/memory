## Exact degree and slot accounting
Let F(x)=sum_{k=0}^{65535} a_k x^k in F_65537[x], with arbitrary public coefficients a_k (the B175 test reference independently inspects 65,536 coefficients). Write H=8192 and eight blocks B_j(x)=sum_{i=0}^{H-1} a_{jH+i}x^i, so exact algebraically:
    F(x)=sum_{j=0}^{7} x^{jH} B_j(x).
This expansion is an identity for every x in F_65537, including x=0 and nonzero roots; it has no carries as an identity in the plaintext field. The proposed direct linear evaluator needs to supply the encrypted monomials x^k for ALL coefficient indices with a_k != 0. For generic arbitrary coefficients, a_65535 !=0 and exponent 65535 is mandatory.
The stock physical B158 layout m=1,r=32768,N=32768, per findings/boot-B158-generalized-evalpower-family-2026-10-28.md, produces P_r=(1,x,...,x^32767) in one full-row ciphertext. This is insufficient to directly represent 65536 independent power positions in ONE such ciphertext. A two-ciphertext, separated upper/lower block basis could logically hold the full range; it is not supplied by the existing one-ciphertext source interface and requires generating/transporting the upper half plus additional public-linear work and keys. This fact is a stock-interface/coverage bound, not an information-theoretic no-go for all encodings.
To create a complete encrypted P_65536 using ordinary multiplicative arithmetic, depth >= ceil(log2(65535))=16 (and the B158 32768 full-row basis itself requires 15 multiplications). This lower bound counts the depth of generating the highest monomial from an input of degree 1 with additive/linear operations and binary ct-ct products. It is not a general lower bound for arbitrary bootstrapping/GSW mechanisms.
Moreover pinned stock full-row B158 no-rescale SI recurrence fails already in the fused stage k4096->8192: required_logQ=665 versus 649 with 32768 mismatches. That was independently raw-artifact-audited. Thus the proposed direct recurrence is NOT a valid candidate for fixed-Q complete power construction. This does not assert alternative constructions always fail or imply a failure for the passed B164 half-basis.
A direct linear coefficient combination cannot repair an already incorrect power basis for arbitrary coefficients: choose F(x)=x^k for a failing k to isolate that basis coordinate. A complete correctness claim for ALL coefficient vectors therefore requires correctness of every invoked monomial component; fixed CASE1 coefficient cancellations are insufficient.
PS block representation as used in B175 trades a compact baby basis and encrypted giant selector for one final bilinear operation. Full coefficient expansion removes that operator only by reintroducing the full high-degree power burden.

## Cost and novelty ceiling
Removing one final SI ct-ct is the only certain structural saving. The construction increases live power-basis scope from H=8192 to 65536 monomials; a one-ciphertext 32768-slot representation is impossible as specified, and adding a second ciphertext requires an unverified alternative source pathway. The existing B163 direct full-row implementation cannot even complete a 32768 power basis at fixed Q. Hence there is no source-validated whole-system correctness and no end-to-end performance claim.
The approach is ordinary public-coefficient polynomial evaluation using an expanded power table (classical dense evaluation), already conceptually known. At most it changes an evaluation tradeoff. Without a novel basis-generation invariant and positive complete key/noise/memory/rotation/runtime ceiling it cannot pass paper contribution gates.
