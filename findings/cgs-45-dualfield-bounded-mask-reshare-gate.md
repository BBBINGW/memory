## Hypothesis and verdict

H-CGS45: A Gaussian coefficient z already privately Shamir-shared in the 640-bit computation prime field F_P, and known to lie in [-H,H] with negligible exceptional probability, can be converted interactively into a fresh Shamir sharing over target F_q without publishing z or pre-storing O(LDec) cross-field randomness, while preserving ANY T-of-N active subset. This is FALSE for naive local reduction of old shares (CGS44), but PASS for the explicit new interactive dual-field-mask protocol below under semi-honest static t<T corruptions and authenticated private channels. No universal cross-field conversion impossibility or new primitive claimed.

## Exact protocol [DERIVATION]

Fix an active set A of size a>=T and designate any T members B within it as mask generators. Assume q and P are distinct primes with Shamir interpolation denominators invertible, and each Gaussian coordinate z has a degree-(T-1) input sharing in F_P, with abs(z)<=H. Choose mask range M and integer computation prime P with P>2H+T(M-1).

For each j in B, independently and privately sample integer r_j uniformly from 0,...,M-1, create independent random degree-(T-1) Shamir polynomials with constants r_j mod P and r_j mod q, and privately send TWO shares, one in each field, to each other active party. This requires NEW authenticated confidential interparty channels, fresh masks every session, and no trusted long-term per-sid dealer.

Each active party locally adds to its F_P sharing of z the constant H and its two-field mask shares. Open/reconstruct PUBLIC y in F_P, satisfying AS INTEGERS y=z+H+sum_j r_j with no field wrap. Each active party computes its NEW F_q Shamir share as the public constant (y-H mod q) MINUS the sum of its received F_q mask shares. Since every mask polynomial has degree below T, the new output is a degree-(T-1) Shamir polynomial with constant z mod q. Hence ANY qualified subset of the active parties can reconstruct exactly z mod q, including subsets different from the T mask generators. For source R_q, do this independently on each polynomial coefficient, using same session party IDs and one degree-(T-1) polynomial per coefficient.

Static semi-honest privacy: With fewer than T corrupted parties there is at least one honest generator. Shares of its mask received by corrupted parties in BOTH fields are independently uniformly random owing to fresh independent Shamir slopes; therefore they reveal nothing about the honest r_j. Conditional on corrupt masks and the other view, the opened y includes an independent length-M uniform interval shifted by z. For any two possible z,z' in [-H,H], total variation of publicly opened y distributions is at most min(1,abs(z-z')/M)<=2H/M; adding other honest random masks can only reduce distance. The destination F_q Shamir sharing is fresh uniform conditional on z because at least one q-mask polynomial has uniform coefficients. This is NOT a malicious or adaptive composition proof and does not cover the original PartDec transcript of Algorithm5.

## Exact online data and scope

Every active contributor privately sends one F_P and one F_q mask share per coefficient to each OTHER active party: T(a-1) values of each type. Every active party broadcasts one F_P masked y share, a additional large values per coefficient. These are TWO NEW dependent online message phases: mask distribution and masked opening, in addition to CGS41–44 interactive sampling and the source two-round ServerDec/PartDec protocol. Extra private authenticated channels and semihonest guarantees are NEW requirements.
