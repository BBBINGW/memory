## ONE minimal arithmetic-circuit derivation [DERIVATION]
Assume common public sample ordering k=1..N (alignment ALREADY solved), each of three parties Pi holds only Yi[k]∈{0,1}; input bits can be secret shared in a sufficiently large prime field F with characteristic >N. Designated output is SEVEN exact scalar raw sums:
  S1=sum_k Y1[k], S2=sum_k Y2[k], S3=sum_k Y3[k];
  S12=sum_k Y1[k]Y2[k], S13=sum_k Y1[k]Y3[k], S23=sum_k Y2[k]Y3[k];
  S123=sum_k Y1[k]Y2[k]Y3[k].
For each record k, compute under sharing t12=Y1*Y2 (1 mult), t13=Y1*Y3 (1), t23=Y2*Y3 (1), t123=t12*Y3 (1); then add all t's locally as shares, reconstruct ONLY the seven desired totals to the designated receiver. This requires EXACTLY 4N secret multiplications FOR THIS DIRECT CIRCUIT, 2 multiplication depths, plus local additions and input sharing; 7 final field elements (+known N). Standard secure multiplication of secret-shared field elements is provided by Beaver triples under the chosen security/preprocessing assumptions; nothing new cryptographically. 4N is NOT a lower bound and NOT a measured communication figure. A secure multiparty circuit can reveal only aggregates to a designated receiver in the semi-honest model, but DOES NOT ensure DP or that the aggregate output itself is innocuous.
Normalize by N. The seven moments are exactly the needed means, pairs and third raw moment; center algebraically to compute covariances Cij and M123 for C03's latent q inversion. If the triple outputs are independent conditional on true label, positively oriented and all population joint moments consistently estimated, q is identifiable in principle. Finite-N estimation, q~0 instability and privacy output are separate unresolved tasks.

CRITICAL output privacy sanity: exact publication of seven raw moment counts is EQUIVALENT (by Boolean Möbius inversion) to releasing the entire EIGHT-cell joint contingency table of (Y1,Y2,Y3). Let m_S = S_S/N and m_empty=1; for a particular binary pattern, its empirical probability is an alternating linear sum of the corresponding moments; e.g.
 p111=m123,
 p110=m12-m123,
 p100=m1-m12-m13+m123,
 p000=1-m1-m2-m3+m12+m13+m23-m123.
Thus 'only 7 scalars' should NEVER be called differentially private; it can expose rare patterns when N small. The output's disclosure policy must be specified. As a simple baseline with fixed N and replace-one record event-level adjacency, the 7-dimensional vector of normalized raw moments has L1-sensitivity <=7/N (attained between 000 and 111), so Laplace noise with per-coordinate scale 7/(N*epsilon) is a standard *central epsilon-DP* option. It entails severe utility/conditioning costs at tiny q and is NOT automatically a scheme for secure distributed DP randomness or user-level DP when one person appears multiple times. This is a known Laplace mechanism, not a new primitive. As a separate output design, computing q privately and releasing only q might leak less but involves more arithmetic/precision and remains non-DP unless protected.

Boundary and obstruction:
- Matched per-example predictions are essential for this exact moment approach. If sites have unrelated samples or only marginals and no linkable identity, the seven moments are not information-theoretically available without an additional anchor, new shared probes, or probabilistic assumptions. This is NOT a crypto impossibility when alignment is permitted; PSI/MPC handles private identifiers with explicit trust and cost assumptions, and PSI itself cannot create statistical pairing where no common candidates exist.
- Standard MPC guarantees secrecy against its specified adversary *up to released function output*; not DP, not truthfulness of verifier bits, not malicious robustness unless validated. Prio SNIPs permit some syntactic validity checks, but not validity of the underlying semantic label.
- Practical sample alignment, secure computation cost, candidate-input privacy, imperfect conditional independence, DP calibration/rare prevalence finite sample confidence and repeated time privacy budget are NOT solved by 4N multiplication toy. Full linked author proof for 2020 PS3I was unavailable; do NOT claim actual 3-party complexity or benchmark.
