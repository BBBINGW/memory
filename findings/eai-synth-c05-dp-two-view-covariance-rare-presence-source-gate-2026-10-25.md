## One rigorous sufficient DP test [DERIVATION]
IID aligned candidate items k=1,...,N with hidden H_k~Bern(q). Two validators (Y1,Y2)∈{0,1} have unknown `f_i=P(Y_i=1|H=0)`, `s_i=P(Y_i=1|H=1)`. Assume CONDITIONAL JOINT independence of these TWO Y_i given H, with known orientation/separation `Delta_i=s_i-f_i>=gamma>0` for i=1,2. No labeled calibration required to RUN the test, but a valid gamma lower bound is an EXTERNAL ASSUMPTION (not recovered). Only test
 H0: q=0
 versus H1: p<=q<=1/2
where 0<p<=1/2 is a user-specified rare-presence gap; it does NOT promise exact q or handling q~1.

Population covariance C = E(Y1Y2)-E(Y1)E(Y2)=q(1-q)Delta1Delta2, by conditional independence and law of total covariance. Under H0, C=0 (even if f_i unknown). Under H1, C>=p gamma²/2 because q(1-q)>=q/2>=p/2. So a covariance threshold tau=p gamma²/4 separates two cases.

Define per-item three vector g(y1,y2)=(y1,y2,y1*y2)∈{0,1}³, sample average (`uhat,vhat,what`). Under REPLACE-ONE PAIR neighboring N-sized datasets, its L1 sensitivity is exactly 3/N (attained 00 vs 11). Release three coordinates with independent Lap(3/(N epsilon)) noise. Standard Laplace mechanism makes this vector **pure epsilon-DP** centrally; release only the decision `D=1{wtilde - utilde*vtilde>tau}` by postprocessing, thus final decision pure epsilon-DP (not an MPC/distributed-DP claim).

For any beta∈(0,1), simultaneous Hoeffding for 3 bounded sample means:
 Pr(max_coord |empirical-population|>a)<=6exp(-2N a²).
Take a=sqrt(ln(12/beta)/(2N)), giving sampling bad-event <=beta/2.
Three independent Laplace noise coordinates with scale b=3/(N epsilon) obey
 Pr(max_coord |noise|>b ln(6/beta))<=beta/2.
On joint success (>=1-beta), each released coordinate is within h=a+b ln(6/beta) of its population mean. With true u,v∈[0,1], |`wtilde-utilde*vtilde` - (w-uv)| <=3h+h² <=4h when h<=1. Requiring h<=p gamma²/32 guarantees error<=p gamma²/8, strictly less than separation margin tau=p gamma²/4. A CONSERVATIVE sufficient condition (by a<=p gamma²/64 and noise<=p gamma²/64) is
 N >= max( 2048 ln(12/beta)/(p² gamma^4), 192 ln(6/beta)/(epsilon p gamma²) ).
Then BOTH type-I and type-II errors are <=beta under any distribution satisfying each composite hypothesis and independence/separation assumptions. This upper sample bound is O((p^-2 gamma^-4 + (epsilon p gamma²)^-1) log(1/beta)). It is an easy sufficient bound; constants huge and not performance-efficient. It exhibits classic statistical and DP noise terms and requires only TWO jointly evaluated validators, not three unknown confusion-matrix inversions. Does not contradict C02 two-point binary FPR p^-2 sample necessity, which remains the dominant statistical term at fixed gamma. The verifier output may itself be private; the central DP adjacency is per matched RECORD, and independent accuracy sampling is an assumption separate from worst-case DP.
