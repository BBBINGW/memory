## ONE exact source-aligned cost derivation [DERIVATION]
Let T positive integer ROUNDS, each generates m>0 equal token-length candidate examples; student each time reinitializes (as Kessler §5.2) and trains accumulated data for exactly E epochs with same per-example compute cost c_s>0 (these equal-length/epochs are additional CLEAR model assumptions). At round t (1-based), accumulated corpus has tm records, thus student-example passes =E m t. Total over rounds:
 N_total=E m sum_{t=1}^T t=Em T(T+1)/2;
 compare one FINAL-only E-epoch SFT over mT records: N_one=E m T.
So ratio R=(T+1)/2; T=10 => R=5.5. For T=5 =>3.0, T=20=>10.5. This is a BUDGET COUNTING identity, not a theorem on complexity-optimal implementation. If E_t, sequence lengths, eval checkpoint cost, teacher output lengths vary: sum actual tokens and FLOPs, not same factor. The reported student-data efficiency plots (teacher tokens, final synthetic count) must not be conflated with *same total compute B*.

A full explicit cost book can be written, WITHOUT CLAIM OF NOVELTY:
