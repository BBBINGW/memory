## Exact mathematical model / derivation
Let P=p0*p1, gcd(p0,p1)=1, p0≠p1; CRT encoding E(x)=(center(x mod p0), center(x mod p1)), center gives unique integer in [-pi/2,pi/2). Let π be a two-block slot swap. The induced reinterpreted plaintext operation is T=CRT∘reduce_to_original_moduli∘π∘E, i.e. T(x)=CRT(center(x mod p1) mod p0, center(x mod p0) mod p1). The **physical CKKS slot rotation is linear as a complex/integer-vector operation**, but T is not a homomorphism of the integer plaintext quotient.
Proof: A unital ring endomorphism f:Z_P→Z_P MUST fix the image of integer 1 and therefore all elements because Z_P is generated by 1. Thus the only unital ring endomorphism is identity. T(1)=1, yet T(3)≠3 in example, so T cannot be a unital endomorphism. One could also directly falsify additive/multiplicative laws.

## Reproducible small-parameter counterexample
Take p0=5, p1=7, P=35. For x=3, centered residues E(3)=(-2,3). Swapping gives (3,-2), interpreted under original modulus assignment (5,7): T(3)=CRT(3 mod5,-2 mod7)=33 mod35.
Take y=3. Original sum x+y=6 yields E(6)=(1,-1); swap=(-1,1); T(6)=CRT(4 mod5,1 mod7)=29. Whereas T(3)+T(3)=33+33=31 mod35. Hence T(x+y)≠T(x)+T(y).
For products: x*y=9, E(9)=(-1,2), swap=(2,-1), T(9)=CRT(2 mod5,6 mod7)=27. But T(3)*T(3)=33^2=4 mod35. Hence T(xy)≠T(x)T(y).
Exact exhaustive 35²=1225 pairs: 522 additive failures and 532 multiplicative failures under T; no numerical FHE approximation or cryptographic security assumption.
