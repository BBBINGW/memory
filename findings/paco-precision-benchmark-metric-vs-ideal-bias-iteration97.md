## Source-matched IDEAL model (DERIVED, not FHE bench)

In hypothetical perfect blind-rotate/partial-CTs/product/conversion stages, only remaining error is small-angle recovery `g(X)=q/(2π)*sin(2πX/q)` rounded to nearest integer. Define ideal integer error `e_ideal(X)=|X−round(g(X))|`; predicted coefficient metric exactly author's LOG-of-individual-coefficients statistic:
`P_ideal=E[log2(|X|+1)−log2(e_ideal(X)+1)]`.
Contrast I96 report where worst-case `−log2(E(p)/p)=11.28/21.28` and hypothetical RMS proxy 12.69/22.69 were NOT the author's statistic.
