## Mathematical test (exact)

For second HMux gadget column, source encrypts digit-basis beta messages with small independent errors e_i and evaluator decomposes the input b into L=9 base-2^52 digits. Error from this column is `sum_i digit_i(b)*e_i`; for |e_i|<=E, worst-case coefficient norm bound `<= L*(2^52-1)*E` in the scalar constant-polynomial subcase.

Candidate encrypts beta once with error e and publicly multiplies by the **raw**, un-decomposed coefficient b. Decryption error = b*e, which is correct *algebraically*, but if b ranges over 468-bit source ciphertext coefficients, one permitted value is b=2^468-1 and a valid nonzero small key-error realization is e=1. Working in source key modulus 2^520, the product b*e is much smaller than 2^519 so does **not** wrap around or disappear mod 2^520. The original gadget digit bound at E=1 is `9*(2^52-1)`; ratio `(2^468-1)/(9*(2^52-1)) > 2^412`. Even comparing one digit gives an amplification of ~2^416. After shared fixed normalization/division (e.g. auxiliary P=2^52), this ratio remains. At P=2^52, raw candidate absolute noise is ~2^416 vs source gadget column noise ~9 units. Thus original noise budget guarantee cannot be inherited at the SAME physical modulus/ordinary-beta encryption-error law. This is a robust constant-ring legal ciphertext input counterexample for H228.

Scope: all-errors-one is one legal nonzero e realization, not a probability claim; arbitrary constant coefficient ciphertext body b can be represented by a trivial valid GLWE ciphertext with (mask=0,body=b). The intended SHIP actual bootstrap may restrict input ciphertext distributions and correlated a,b; **that restriction has not been proved sufficient to exclude large b**. For normal nontrivial encryptions a,b have full-width coefficients anyway. The counterexample is for original per-HMux key general input contract, not a proof all application-specialized compression is impossible. Other main-column, automorphism and rounding noise omitted; their presence cannot justify claiming the full baseline bound from this wrong comparison. A scheme with larger physical modulus, more guard precision, constrained plaintext bodies, randomized correlated cancellations, or a new decomposition remains outside H228 and needs separate cost/security gate.

## Security/novelty and decision

Separating a message-input gadget column and multiplying by an ordinary encrypted beta is a direct linear algebra rewrite in plaintext semantics, not itself a new FHE primitive. Noise amplification is the standard reason gadget decomposition/key switching is used for full-width public coefficients, consistent with original paper §5.1 gadget-decomposition contract.
