**[ONE TEST]** Take a valid HFP ciphertext `F` (or the output of `CarryPropagateFloat(F)`) with properly encoded exponent vector `ce`, marginal post-refresh noise E in a selected exponent limb, and feed **the same ciphertext F twice** to `Addition(F,F)`.
