## The no-go lemma (formal statement and proof)
Let R=R1×R2 be a nontrivial product of commutative rings with central idempotents e=(0,1) and 1−e=(1,0). Consider ANY finite system of polynomials `f_j(x,w)=0` in unknowns `x∈R^t`, existential auxiliary witness `w∈R^m`, with arbitrary fixed coefficients from R. Let L={x:∃w, ∀j f_j(x,w)=0}.
Claim: L=L1×L2 under CRT, with Li the projected algebraic existential relations.
Proof 1: polynomial evaluation and each zero equality split coordinatewise, and any independent field-component witnesses w1,w2 CRT-glue into one w∈R^m. Hence equality of sets.
Equivalent patching proof: if (x0,w0), (x1,w1) satisfy all equations, define `x*= (1−e)x0+e x1`, `w*=(1−e)w0+e w1`. For any polynomial f,
 `f(x*,w*)=(1−e)f(x0,w0)+e f(x1,w1)=0`
because e²=e, (1−e)²=1−e and e(1−e)=0. Works for ALL finite numbers/degrees of equations, any auxiliary ring witnesses and any coefficients in R.
If scalar table T_beta contains 0 and 1, completeness requires x0=0 and x1=1 to be legal, with accepting witness pairs. Patching yields x*=e, which has projections (0,1) but is **not** an element of diagonal table {0*1_R,1*1_R}; for beta>2 and beta<pmin, e likewise not any diagonal integer digit. Thus no pure R-algebraic existential-equation gadget can characterize the diagonal scalar table with completeness for both 0 and1. The result applies to any number of factors by induction.
More generally, ring-polynomial P with P(0)=P(1)=0 also has `P(e)=(1−e)P(0)+e P(1)=0`. Therefore added polynomial relations such as `b(b−1)=0`, per-factor Frobenius constraints, or per-factor trace-to-base-field constraints do not exclude the attack.
[EXACT SCOPE] This is a mathematical limit on **positive-existential ring polynomial equations**, and on extensions of the PIOP whose gates/intermediate witnesses/verification conditions remain CRT-local and accept honest digits. It is NOT an impossibility theorem for all cryptographic protocols, proof systems with external integer encodings, equality/inequality checks not expressible as zero polynomial equations, global normalization, or genuinely non-CRT-local coupling operations. Polynomial commitments as components may add external hashes but binding by itself is not semantic digit membership.
[INTERACTIVE NUANCE] For a PIOP consisting only of R-algebraic constraints, true honest strategies for 0 and 1 can be CRT-glued for every verifier ring challenge; a correlated exceptional-set sampling distribution does not break algebraic identities true for all challenges. A protocol with extra non-algebraic global checks is outside the lemma.

## What kind of repairs remain possible (not developed)
A repair must introduce at least one check that does NOT factor as independent polynomial equations under the relevant CRT decomposition (not just per-factor Frobenius or ring Boolean selectors). Example candidate class: commit once to a common *external integer/bit representation* of each digit, separately prove that SAME representation reduces to every prime modulus component, and connect it to ring witness h; requires integer-lift/modular congruence arguments and likely costs. Another candidate class: a genuinely cross-component scalar-subring/diagonal-table relation implemented outside pure R-polynomial constraints.
A ring-only Boolean proof of a shared index bit still permits idempotent value e and therefore is **not enough**. A ring-only polynomial-commitment to that alleged shared index is also subject to the same obstacle unless its verifier supplies external cross-component equality/semantics.
Distinct underlying prime characteristic prevents an injective field-homomorphic embedding of the product ring into one field; a common outside field must encode integer lifts and congruences rather than reuse a free ring homomorphism. Consequently no performance win should be claimed without precise computation/communication/modulus cost.
Neither a new practical cross-component SNARK gadget nor a new complexity lower bound is proven: the no-go only excludes an explicitly defined class.
