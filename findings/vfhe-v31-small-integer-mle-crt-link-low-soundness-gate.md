## Decisive explicit adversarial digit pair [EXACT DERIVATION]

For each of 12 **pairs of Boolean input indices**, define a base-2×2 digit DIFFERENCE table delta2(u,v): δ2(00)=−1, δ2(01)=δ2(10)=0, δ2(11)=+1. It is realizable by legal digit vectors with values 0/1 and therefore accepted by each individual t_beta table even for beta4. Define global delta=δ2 tensor δ2 tensor ... (12 factors) on {0,1}^24; delta_i in {-1,0,1} and has 2^12=4096 nonzero positions. Let a_i=max(delta_i,0), b_i=max(−delta_i,0). Both length 2^24 digit vectors are legal 0/1 digits. The corresponding MLE difference equals

`P_a(r)-P_b(r) = PRODUCT_{j=0}^{11} [ r_{2j}r_{2j+1}−(1−r_{2j})(1−r_{2j+1}) ] = PRODUCT_{j=0}^{11} [r_{2j}+r_{2j+1}−1]`.

For a challenge pair r_{2j},r_{2j+1} drawn independently uniformly from {-1,2}, the factor is −3 at (-1,-1), +3 at (2,2), and exactly ZERO at mixed pairs (-1,2),(2,-1). Thus all 12 factors avoid zero with probability `(1/2)^12=1/4096`; otherwise the two different native MLE integer outputs EXACTLY MATCH. One additional no-wrap centered-lift MLE check **misses this legitimate locally range-constrained cross-prime disagreement with probability 4095/4096 ≈ 99.97558594%**. This contradicts H-V31 >=1/2 detection. The mismatch differs across primes at 4096 of 2^24 digits, yet is perfectly valid locally; CRT combines them into a unique ring oracle value at every position.
